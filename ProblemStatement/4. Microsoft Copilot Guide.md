## What are agents?

Agents use AI to automate and execute business processes, working alongside or on behalf of a person, team or organization.

Agents vary in complexity and capability, ranging from **Simple** (Retrieval, Task) to **Advanced** (Autonomous), depending on your need. Example agent archetypes referenced in the deck include: IT Helpdesk agent, Device Refresh agent, Lead Gen agent, Project Tracker agent, Budget Management agent, and Customer Support agent.

## Connect agents to each other, regardless of where they're built

Agents built with Copilot Studio can connect to agents/AI services from:

* Azure AI Foundry
* Semantic Kernel
* Amazon Web Services
* Google Cloud Platform
* IBM Cloud
* OpenAI
* LangChain
* Other...

**Copilot is the UI for AI** — Copilot, Copilot Agents, and the Copilot Control System work together.

**Copilot Studio is the platform for AI agents** — Copilot / Copilot Studio / Agents, governed by the Copilot Control System.

### Copilot Studio — 3 pillars

1. **Built on Microsoft. Connected to Everything.** — Achieve goals faster by enabling agents with your company's data and third-party systems.
2. **Build any agent. From simple to sophisticated.** — Reduce development time and costs on custom agents by selecting from your choice of models, knowledge, tools, and channels.
3. **Govern every agent. Secure every action.** — Innovate at scale by managing data access, agent sprawl, compliance and risk.
