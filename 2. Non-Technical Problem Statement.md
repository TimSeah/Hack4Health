**Non-Technical Innovation Track**

## **Challenge Brief**

### **Increasing Adult Vaccination Uptake through AI-Enabled Innovation**

## **Background**

Vaccination remains one of the most effective public health interventions. However, adult vaccination uptake remains below recommended levels despite the availability of safe and effective vaccines.

Many eligible adults:

* are unaware of recommended vaccines;
* underestimate their personal risk;
* forget booster schedules;
* face behavioural or logistical barriers to vaccination; or
* do not receive timely recommendations during routine healthcare visits.

Healthcare providers also face challenges in consistently identifying eligible patients and recommending vaccinations during busy consultations.

Parkway Shenton is looking for innovative, patient-centred solutions that leverage artificial intelligence and digital technology to improve adult vaccination uptake at scale.

## **Business Challenge**

**How might we design an AI-enabled strategy that significantly increases adult vaccination uptake while remaining practical, scalable, and sustainable for deployment across Parkway Shenton?**

Your proposal should demonstrate how AI or digital technologies can create measurable improvements in vaccination uptake by addressing patient behaviour, clinical workflows, healthcare communications, or service delivery.

The focus is **not** on building complex AI systems, but on developing a compelling business solution supported by appropriate technology.

**Teams May Propose Innovations Involving**

* behavioural science;
* public education;
* healthcare communications;
* service design;
* community engagement;
* partnerships;
* digital marketing;
* incentives;
* workflow redesign;
* patient empowerment;
* policy recommendations;
* AI-assisted identification of eligible patients;
* automated vaccination-history checks;
* clinical prompts, reminders or decision-support tools;
* integration of vaccination recommendations into routine clinic workflows; and
* patient-facing digital reminders or engagement tools.

**Solutions can Ideally**

* increase awareness of recommended adult vaccinations;
* improve accessibility and convenience;
* encourage timely vaccination;
* strengthen patient trust;
* reduce missed vaccination opportunities;
* identify eligible patients during routine clinic visits;
* provide timely prompts to doctors or clinic staff;
* avoid unnecessary or duplicate vaccinations by checking available vaccination records;
* integrate smoothly into existing clinical workflows;
* minimise additional administrative burden on healthcare staff;
* protect patient privacy and comply with relevant data-governance requirements; and
* be scalable across Parkway Shenton and IHH Healthcare Singapore.

**Judging Criteria**

* Understanding of the problem
* Creativity
* Practicality
* Evidence-based thinking
* Potential public health impact
* Appropriate and meaningful use of AI or software
* Ease of implementation
* Integration with existing clinic workflows
* Scalability
* Quality of presentation
